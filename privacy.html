Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective 20 October 2026 · 2026 年 10 月 20 日生效

What PageFuse processes

PageFuse processes the PDF files you select and the project information you create on your Apple device. It keeps imported copies, Master PDFs, unfinished merge drafts, and revision history in local app storage so you can review and update a project later.

Accounts, analytics, and servers

PageFuse does not require an account. The current app does not send your PDF contents, project names, or usage analytics to a PageFuse server and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Files and sharing

You select PDFs through Apple's Files picker. Files stored in iCloud Drive or another provider remain subject to that provider's behavior. When you choose Export or Share, the system share interface passes the exported PDF to the destination you select; that destination may have its own privacy policy.

Control and deletion

You can delete saved projects and their copies in PageFuse. You can also remove the app to delete its local app data, subject to Apple device backup and file-provider behavior. PageFuse does not offer cloud account deletion because it has no account service.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email freesysck@outlook.com.

PageFuse 处理什么

PageFuse 在你的 Apple 设备上处理你选取的 PDF 和创建的项目信息。导入的副本、Master PDF、未完成的合并草稿和版本历史保存在 App 本地存储中，方便日后复核和更新。

账户、分析与服务器

PageFuse 无需账户。当前 App 不会把 PDF 内容、项目名称或使用分析数据发送到 PageFuse 服务器，也没有广告或第三方分析 SDK。

文件与分享

你通过 Apple“文件”选择器选取 PDF。存于 iCloud Drive 或其他服务的文件仍受其提供方的行为约束。当你选择导出或分享时，系统分享界面会把 PDF 交给你选定的目标；该目标可能有自己的隐私政策。

控制与删除

你可以在 PageFuse 中删除已保存的项目及副本。删除 App 也会移除其本地数据，但仍受 Apple 设备备份和文件提供方行为影响。PageFuse 没有云账户，因此无需账户删除流程。

联系

隐私或支持问题请发送至 freesysck@outlook.com。
